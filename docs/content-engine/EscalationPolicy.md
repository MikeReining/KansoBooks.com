# Autonomous Content Engine Escalation Policy

**Status:** Phase 1 contract seed
**Authority:** Escalation rules for content-engine agents and validators.

## Escalate To The Founder

Escalate only when the decision changes product semantics, trust semantics,
security posture, access policy, or roadmap priority.

Required escalation:

- product truth conflict across canonical docs or truth files
- local-first or cloud-data boundary ambiguity
- AI truth boundary ambiguity
- tax, legal, payroll, sales tax, audit, filing, or entity-specific claim that
  cannot be safely generalized
- competitor, pricing, crawler, platform, legal, or standard source conflict
- credentials, billing, provider, CI, deployment, or branch-protection failure
  that cannot be mechanically fixed
- proposal to broaden autonomous agent write access
- proposal to publish outside the approved content paths

## Do Not Escalate

Do not escalate for routine topic choice, article drafting, metadata
optimization, internal links, source formatting, refreshes, or publication when
all gates pass.

## Failure Output

Every escalation report must include:

- content item or sprint ID
- failed gate
- exact unsupported or conflicting claim
- source paths or URLs checked
- narrow decision needed
- recommended safe fallback
