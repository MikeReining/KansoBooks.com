# Autonomous Content Engine Vision

**Status:** Phase 1 contract seed
**Authority:** Operational content vision for the Autonomous Content Engine.
Subordinate to `docs/phases/AutonomousContentEngine.md` and the canonical
KansoBooks product docs.

KansoBooks content exists to help one reader job:

```text
Get the books ready.
Know what is still uncertain.
Send the accountant a package that explains itself.
```

The engine should publish only pages that make this job easier. It is not a
generic accounting blog, a tax advice surface, or a cloud-books positioning
vehicle.

Binding defaults:

- Founder approval is not a publishing gate.
- Publishing is not allowed in Phase 1.
- Product truth lives in `content/_truth/` and canonical docs.
- Every publishable page needs metadata, a canonical job, and a claim manifest.
- Every serious page needs decision support and a proof boundary.
- Training-crawler policy is a future business decision, not a silent default.

Editorial defaults:

- solve a distinct reader job
- connect to books-readiness or buying intent
- include a proof, checklist, decision, template, or worked example
- state professional boundaries plainly
- resolve factual claims to a source, truth file, or canonical Kanso doc
- avoid tax, legal, audit, or CPA advice
- preserve the local-first and AI-truth boundaries
