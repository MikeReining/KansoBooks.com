Send your accountant the records that explain the numbers, not a pile of files and a note that says "let me know if anything is missing." For most small businesses, the useful tax-time handoff includes the period scope, source statements, reviewed bookkeeping reports, reconciliation notes, unusual-item questions, owner notes, and any documents your accountant specifically requested.

The goal is not to decide tax treatment yourself. The goal is to make the bookkeeping package clear enough that your accountant can see what is done, what is missing, and what needs professional judgment.

KansoBooks treats tax-time prep as a confidence problem. AI can help organize the routine work, but tax-time relief comes from checked records, visible uncertainty, and a package that does not force your accountant to reconstruct the year from scattered exports.

## Tax-Time Handoff List

| Send this | What it should answer | Warning sign |
|---|---|---|
| Scope note | Which year, business, accounts, and months are included? | You send files without saying what period they cover. |
| Bank and card statements | Do the bookkeeping records have source files behind them? | Some accounts are connected in software, but no statements or exports are saved. |
| Payment processor exports | Do Stripe, PayPal, Shopify, Square, or similar accounts match the income records? | Gross deposits appear in the bank without processor detail. |
| Loan, financing, and owner activity notes | Which items may need accountant review? | Loan payments, owner draws, contributions, transfers, or financing fees are buried in normal expense categories. |
| Reviewed P&L and balance sheet when applicable | What do the reviewed reports say for the period? | Reports exist, but no one knows whether source files and balances were checked. |
| Reconciliation status | Which balances tie to statements, and which differences remain open? | Transactions are categorized, but ending balances were never checked. |
| Evidence index | Where are the statements, receipts, invoices, contracts, and notes? | Documents are spread across email, downloads, and screenshots with no index. |
| Open questions | What exactly needs accountant judgment? | You send "please review everything" because the uncertainty was never named. |
| Prior-year or carryforward notes when requested | What does this year depend on from earlier work? | The accountant has to guess what changed from last year. |

You do not need to make the package perfect before sending it. You do need to make uncertainty visible. A short list of "I am not sure about these five items" is much better than a clean-looking report that hides questions in totals.

## What This Helps You Decide

Use this list when you are asking, "What should I send so my accountant can actually help me?"

It helps you separate three different jobs:

| Job | Owner-safe work | Accountant work |
|---|---|---|
| Organize records | Gather statements, exports, reports, notes, and source files. | Tell you which additional documents they need for your situation. |
| Check bookkeeping readiness | Confirm accounts, balances, categories, and open questions are visible. | Decide treatment for uncertain or specialized items. |
| Prepare for filing | Send a clear package and specific questions. | Prepare, review, advise on, or file according to their professional role. |

That separation matters because many owners get stuck trying to solve the wrong problem. You may not be able to answer every tax question. You can still send a cleaner package that makes the next professional step easier.

## The 10-Minute Exit Test

Before you send the package, pick one month and one unusual transaction.

Can you show your accountant:

| Question | Good answer | Not ready yet |
|---|---|---|
| Which source file supports this period? | A named statement, export, or folder entry. | "It is somewhere in the app." |
| Do balances tie out? | The statement total matches, or the difference is listed. | The report looks plausible, but no balance check exists. |
| Why is this unusual item flagged? | The item has a note, evidence file, or accountant question. | It is hidden inside a broad category. |
| What do you need the accountant to decide? | A specific question is written down. | You are hoping they notice the problem. |

If you can do that for one part of the year, repeat the pattern across the rest of the package. If you cannot, pause and make the missing file, mismatch, or question visible before sending.

## What You Can Prove

This handoff list can help show that a tax-time bookkeeping package contains named source files, reviewed reports, reconciliation status, evidence references, and explicit questions for accountant review.

It cannot prove a tax filing position, deduction, entity treatment, payroll treatment, sales tax decision, audit outcome, legal conclusion, or accountant-specific requirement. Those decisions belong with qualified professional judgment. It also cannot make AI-drafted bookkeeping true by itself. AI can prepare and organize; checked evidence and approval decide what is reliable enough to send forward.

## Entity Summary

- Tax-time handoff package: the records, reports, evidence, and questions sent to an accountant before tax preparation or review.
- Scope note: a plain statement of the business, period, accounts, and exclusions included in the package.
- Evidence index: a list that points from reports and questions back to source files, statements, receipts, invoices, contracts, and notes.
- Reconciliation status: whether balances tie to source statements or unresolved differences have been named.
- Open question: anything that still needs owner input or accountant judgment before the records can be relied on.
- AI-drafted bookkeeping: bookkeeping work prepared by AI before validation and approval; useful draft, not financial truth.
