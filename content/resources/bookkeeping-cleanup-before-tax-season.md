If your books are messy before tax season, clean them in this order: collect source files, define the period, check missing accounts, reconcile balances, review transfers and duplicates, explain unusual items, and write down open questions for your accountant. Do not start by polishing reports. Start by making the evidence and uncertainty visible.

The goal is not to solve every tax or accounting question yourself. The goal is to turn a stressful pile of records into a package your accountant can review without guessing what is missing, what was checked, and what still needs a decision.

KansoBooks treats cleanup as a handoff problem, not a cosmetic problem. AI can help draft categories and find patterns, but the useful output is a checked record with source files, review notes, and explicit questions.

## Cleanup Triage Order

| Step | Do this first | Good enough to move on when |
|---|---|---|
| Define the period | Pick the date range and accounts you are cleaning. | The cleanup has a named start date, end date, and account list. |
| Collect source files | Gather statements, exports, receipts, invoices, loan records, and payment processor reports for the period. | Every account in scope has a source file or a written missing-file note. |
| Check missing accounts | Compare the account list against how money actually moved. | Bank, card, loan, cash, and payment processor accounts are either included or named as out of scope. |
| Reconcile balances | Tie ending balances to statements where possible. | Balances match or the differences are listed for review. |
| Review transfers | Look for account-to-account movement before reviewing income and expenses. | Transfers are paired or flagged as unresolved. |
| Review duplicates | Search repeated amounts, vendors, deposits, refunds, fees, and imported batches. | Duplicate-looking activity is removed, merged, or explained. |
| Explain unusual items | Sort by amount and scan one-time, owner, loan, refund, personal-looking, or unclear transactions. | Each unusual item has a note, evidence file, or accountant question. |
| Package open questions | Put unresolved items in one list. | Your accountant can see the question, the source file, and what you already checked. |

This order keeps you from spending hours categorizing transactions that later turn out to be missing, duplicated, or unreconciled.

## What This Helps You Decide

Use this triage when the books feel too messy to finish in one sitting.

It helps answer three practical questions:

1. What has to be cleaned before reports mean anything?
2. What can I safely organize myself?
3. What should be sent forward as an accountant question instead of guessed?

The best cleanup result is a smaller, clearer problem. You should end with checked records, named gaps, and a handoff list, not a vague promise that the books are probably fine.

## The Two-Hour Cleanup Pass

If you only have two hours, spend them on evidence and scope before categories.

| Time | Work | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 20 minutes | Name the period and accounts. | Cleanup scope note. |
| 40 minutes | Collect source files and missing-file notes. | Source-file folder or list. |
| 25 minutes | Check balances, duplicates, and transfers. | Reconciliation and exception notes. |
| 25 minutes | Review the largest and strangest transactions. | Unusual-item list. |
| 10 minutes | Write accountant questions. | Handoff question list. |

If you run out of time, stop with a cleaner package, not a half-polished report. A clear exception list is more useful than hidden uncertainty.

## What You Can Prove

This cleanup order can help prove what records were included, what period was reviewed, which accounts were checked, where balances matched or failed, which transfers and duplicates were reviewed, and which questions remain open.

It cannot prove tax treatment, filing positions, payroll treatment, sales tax treatment, audit conclusions, legal conclusions, or entity-specific decisions. Those belong with qualified professional judgment. It also cannot make AI-drafted cleanup true by itself. AI can prepare the work; source files, checks, and approval decide what belongs in the record.

## Entity Summary

- Bookkeeping cleanup: the work of turning incomplete, messy, or uncertain records into checked records and visible questions.
- Cleanup scope: the period, accounts, and source files included in the cleanup.
- Source file: a statement, export, receipt, invoice, loan record, or report that supports the bookkeeping record.
- Exception list: missing files, mismatches, duplicate risks, transfer issues, unusual items, and open questions.
- Accountant handoff: the package of reports, source files, notes, and questions sent forward for professional review.
